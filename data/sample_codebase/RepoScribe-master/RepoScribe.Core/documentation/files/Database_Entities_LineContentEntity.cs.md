## Class: `LineContentEntity`

Represents a line of content within a content item in the database.

**Purpose:** Stores and manages individual lines of content with their associated metadata.

### Properties

  ### `Content`

  The textual content of the line

  ### `ContentItem`

  The associated ContentItemEntity for this LineContentEntity

  ### `ContentItemEntityId`

  The identifier of the associated ContentItemEntity

  ### `Id`

  Unique identifier for this LineContentEntity instance

  ### `Number`

  The sequence number of this line content
